Alone in the Rain

Behind a quiet, abandoned garden, a small stray kitten named Milo huddles inside a soaked cardboard box — cold, hungry, and utterly alone.

You Found Him

You crouch down and shield him from the storm. For the first time in days, Milo feels warm again — and he's not ready to let you go.

Every Block, a Little Warmth

Every line you clear turns into Warmth — the spark of care that slowly rebuilds this ruined garden, one block at a time.

A Haven for Every Stray

Milo won't be the last stray who finds his way here. Clear puzzles, gather Warmth, and welcome every cat home — one Haven at a time.
