Privacy Policy — Cat Block Haven

Last updated: 2026-08-17

Cat Block Haven ("the Game") is a single-player puzzle game. This policy explains what data the Game does — and does not — handle.

What we collect

Nothing. The Game does not have a server, does not require an account, and does not send any data over the network. There is no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking of any kind.

What's stored on your device

Your progress (level, gold, warmth, recruited cats, furniture, settings) is saved using your browser's local storage, entirely on your own device. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us or anyone else. Uninstalling the app or clearing your browser data will erase it permanently.

Permissions

The Game does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, location, or any other device permission beyond standard local storage for saving progress.

Children's privacy

Because the Game collects no personal data of any kind, it does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes (for example, if online features are added in the future), the update will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent via the contact information listed on the Google Play Store listing for this app.
