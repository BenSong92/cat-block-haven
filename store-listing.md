## Full description (4000 char max)

🐱 A cold, rainy day. A tiny stray kitten named Milo, alone in a cardboard box.
You're the one who brings him in from the rain — and that's where Cat Block Haven begins.

**Clear lines, build a home.**
Cat Block Haven is a relaxing 8x8 block puzzle with a warm twist. Drag polyomino pieces onto the board, clear full rows and columns, and chain combos for big score bursts. Every line you clear earns Warmth — the currency that turns a ruined garden into a cozy sanctuary.

**Recruit 10 cats, each with a real puzzle skill.**
Spend Warmth on furniture to recruit Milo, Cleo, Leo, Tux, Blue, Coco, Mocha, Cloud, Oliver, and Forest — ten cat breeds, each with a distinct personality and an active skill you trigger mid-puzzle:
🐾 Milo's 3x3 Paw Punch blasts a chunk of the board
🎨 Cleo's Color Wipe clears every block of one color
✨ Leo's Cross Laser clears a full row and column at once
💰 Tux's Gold Booster doubles your gold for a few seconds
❄️ Blue's Ice Breaker shatters every obstacle on the board
⚡ Coco speeds up everyone else's skill charge
💗 Mocha grants a free revive when you'd otherwise be stuck
🧹 Cloud tidies up a cluttered board
🎡 Oliver hands you bonus moves
🧲 Forest sweeps up every bonus gem on screen

**An idle sanctuary that keeps working while you're away.**
Recruited cats earn gold passively — check back later and collect what they've saved up.

**500 levels, four chapters, and an endless mode.**
Play through a hand-tuned difficulty curve from a gentle tutorial to advanced combo challenges, with ice blocks and yarn-ball obstacles joining the board as you progress. Clear all 500 levels and Infinite Mode unlocks — no target score, just how far your combos can carry you.

**Made for a quick, cozy session.**
No ads interrupting your puzzle. No forced videos. No pressure. Just a warm little game about a rescued kitten and the friends he brings home.

🔒 Cat Block Haven works entirely on your device — no account, no ads, no data collection.
